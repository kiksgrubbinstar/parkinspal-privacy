# ParkinsPal Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 21, 2026<br>
**Last updated:** September 21, 2026

ParkinsPal ("ParkinsPal," "we," "us," or "our") is a wellness-tracking application that helps users record symptoms, medications, reminders, notes, and optional voice recordings. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the ParkinsPal mobile application (the "App") or contact us for support.

## Privacy at a glance

- ParkinsPal does not require or offer a user account.
- Information entered in the App is stored locally on your device.
- We do not operate a server that receives your symptom logs, medication information, notes, or voice recordings.
- We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, show targeted ads, or track users across apps or websites.
- We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs in the App.
- You control microphone and notification permissions through your device settings.

## 1. Scope

This Policy applies to the ParkinsPal mobile App and support communications sent to us. It does not govern the independent practices of Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, mobile carrier, healthcare provider, or any service you choose to use outside the App.

## 2. Information handled by the App

### Symptom and wellness information

If you choose to create a symptom log, the App stores the log date, shaking or tremor severity, stiffness, slow-movement difficulty, walking difficulty, balance or unsteadiness, fatigue, mood, written notes, and creation time. The App uses these entries to display history and trends on your device.

### Medication and reminder information

If you add a medication, the App stores the medication name, dosage, frequency, scheduled times, reminder preference, local notification identifiers, and creation time. This information is used to display your medication schedule and schedule local reminders.

### Voice recordings

If you choose to record a voice note, the App accesses the microphone after requesting permission and saves the audio file in the App's private storage area on your device. The App also stores a local file reference so the recording can be played from symptom history. We do not receive or listen to these recordings.

### App settings and technical records

The App stores limited settings locally, such as acceptance of the terms, notification preferences, and identifiers needed to manage scheduled notifications. The App does not create an advertising identifier, user profile, or account identifier.

### Support communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you voluntarily provide, such as your email address, message, and any attachments. Please do not send medical records or other sensitive health information by email. Support correspondence is used only to respond, troubleshoot, protect the App, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. Information we do not collect through the App

The App does not transmit to us or our servers:

- symptom, wellness, medication, or reminder information;
- voice recordings or written notes;
- precise or approximate location;
- contacts, photos, videos, browsing history, or search history;
- payment or financial information;
- government identifiers;
- advertising identifiers; or
- data used to track you across apps, services, or websites.

## 4. How information is used

Information stored on your device is processed locally to:

- save and display symptom logs and history;
- calculate and display weekly, monthly, and yearly trends;
- save medication schedules;
- schedule and manage local medication and affirmation reminders;
- record and play optional voice notes; and
- remember basic App settings.

Support information that you send us is used to respond to your request, diagnose problems, maintain security, and comply with law. We do not use health or support information for advertising or automated decision-making.

## 5. Device permissions

### Microphone

Microphone access is used only to create a voice note when you use the recording feature. You may deny or revoke microphone access in your device settings. If access is denied, the rest of the App remains available, but voice recording will not work.

### Notifications

Notification permission is used for medication and daily affirmation reminders. Notifications are scheduled locally using the operating system. You may deny or revoke notification permission in your device settings. Doing so prevents reminders from appearing but does not delete medication information already stored in the App.

ParkinsPal does not request access to location, contacts, calendars, photos, camera, Bluetooth, or HealthKit.

## 6. Storage, transmission, and backups

App information is stored in the App's sandboxed storage on your device, including an on-device SQLite database and local audio files. ParkinsPal does not operate a cloud database or backend that receives this information.

Your operating system or a backup service you enable may include App data in an encrypted device or cloud backup. Those backups are controlled by you and the relevant platform provider, not by ParkinsPal. Review your Apple iCloud, Finder, Google, or device-backup settings if you do not want App data included in backups.

## 7. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, license, or share App health information with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, employers, insurers, healthcare providers, or other third parties. We do not use App information for targeted advertising or cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information may be handled by others only in these limited situations:

- **At your direction:** If you independently show, play, copy, back up, or otherwise disclose information from your device, that disclosure is controlled by you.
- **Platform providers:** Apple, Google, device manufacturers, or mobile carriers may process information as part of operating-system services, App Store distribution, device backups, or diagnostics according to their own privacy policies and your settings.
- **Support providers:** If you email us, our email provider processes the correspondence so we can receive and answer it.
- **Legal and safety reasons:** We may disclose support correspondence if reasonably necessary to comply with law, enforce legal rights, prevent fraud or abuse, or protect users and others. Because App records remain on your device, we generally do not possess App records to disclose.
- **Business transition:** If responsibility for ParkinsPal changes, support records and policy obligations may transfer to the successor, subject to applicable law. On-device App records are not transferred to us because we do not possess them.

Any service provider processing information for us is expected to protect it and use it only for the service requested.

## 8. Retention and deletion

Symptom logs, medication entries, written notes, voice recordings, and settings remain on your device unless changed or removed through available App controls, removed by the operating system, or deleted when you uninstall the App. The current App allows individual medication entries to be deleted. It does not currently provide an in-App control to delete individual symptom logs or a remote account-deletion function because no account or remote copy exists.

To delete all ParkinsPal information stored by the App, uninstall the App from your device. Deleting the App may not delete copies contained in device backups; backup copies are governed by your backup provider's settings and retention practices. We cannot access or remotely delete information stored on your device or in your personal device backup.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business and security records, resolve disputes, and comply with law. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing us, subject to any legal retention obligation.

## 9. Your choices and privacy rights

You may:

- choose whether to enter symptom, medication, note, or voice information;
- deny or revoke microphone and notification permissions in device settings;
- delete individual medication entries using the App;
- delete all locally stored App information by uninstalling the App;
- manage or delete device backups through your platform account; and
- ask questions or request access, correction, or deletion of support correspondence by contacting us.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may provide additional rights regarding personal information we possess, such as access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or appeal. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising a privacy right. Because we do not receive App records, we cannot identify, retrieve, change, or delete those records remotely.

ParkinsPal does not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, so there is no sale or targeted-advertising opt-out mechanism in the App.

## 10. Security

We minimize risk by keeping App information on your device and not operating a backend containing user health records. App information benefits from protections provided by the operating system, such as application sandboxing, device passcodes, and device encryption when enabled. You are responsible for securing your device and backups.

No storage method is completely secure. A lost, unlocked, compromised, or shared device may expose locally stored information. ParkinsPal does not separately encrypt each database field or audio file beyond protections provided by the operating system and your device configuration.

## 11. Health information and medical privacy

ParkinsPal is a direct-to-consumer wellness and self-tracking tool. It is not a healthcare provider, health plan, healthcare clearinghouse, or medical-record system, and using the App does not create a healthcare-provider relationship. ParkinsPal is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, emergencies, or clinical decision-making.

Health privacy laws such as the U.S. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) may not apply to information you enter into a consumer App when the developer is not acting for a HIPAA-covered entity. Your healthcare provider may have separate legal duties for information you choose to share directly with them.

## 12. Children's privacy

ParkinsPal is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. The App's Terms require users to be at least 18 or the age of majority in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the App. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent us personal information in a support message, please contact us so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

## 13. International users

ParkinsPal can be used in different locations, but App records remain on the user's device unless the user enables a platform backup. If you contact us, your support correspondence may be processed in the United States and by the email services used to deliver the message. Where required, we handle such correspondence under applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

## 14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the App, law, or our practices. We will update the date at the top and publish the revised Policy at the same URL. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide additional notice when reasonably required. Continued use after an update means the revised Policy applies to future use.
